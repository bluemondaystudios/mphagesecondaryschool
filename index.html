About the school

A community school with high expectations

Mphage Secondary School serves families in Tafelkop and the surrounding villages near Groblersdal. We are a public school under the Limpopo Department of Education, teaching learners from Grade 8 through to matric.

Our motto, Per Disciplina Excellentiam, means “through discipline, excellence”. We believe that every learner who shows up, works consistently and believes in themselves can achieve great things. Our educators set high standards and celebrate the learners who meet them.

  • Grade 8 to Grade 12 (matric)
  • Learners recognised for achievement each year
  • 81% of our matric class passed in 2024

What we teach beyond the classroom

Six habits of a Mphage learner

  1. 01

    Set SMART goals

    Specific, measurable goals turn big dreams into a plan you can follow every day.

  2. 02

    Believe in yourself

    Confidence grows from effort. We help every learner see what they are capable of.

  3. 03

    Stay focused

    Attention in class and at home is where real learning happens.

  4. 04

    Stay consistent

    Small, steady steps, taken every day, add up to excellent results.

  5. 05

    Your future is in your hands

    The choices learners make today shape the opportunities they have tomorrow.

  6. 06

    Take charge

    We grow leaders who take ownership of their learning and their community.

Results & recognition

Hard work that shows

In 2024, 81% of our Grade 12 learners passed the National Senior Certificate. Behind that number are learners and educators who stayed consistent all year.

Throughout the year we recognise top achievers and committed learners with certificates of appreciation, presented in front of their peers, families and educators.

Admissions 2026

Enrol now for Grades 8–11

Your child deserves the best education. Application forms are available at the school office.

Get directions
  1. 1

    Collect a form

    Visit the school office in Kampeng Section, Boleu, during school hours and ask for a 2026 admission form.

  2. 2

    Complete & attach

    Fill in the form and attach copies of the required documents listed below.

  3. 3

    Return to the school

    Hand in the completed form at the office. The school will contact you about the outcome.

Documents usually required

  • Learner’s birth certificate
  • Latest school report
  • Parent or guardian’s ID
  • Transfer letter from previous school
  • Proof of residence
  • Immunisation card (Grade 8)

Please confirm the full list with the school office when you collect your form. Questions? Call 071 675 4586.
